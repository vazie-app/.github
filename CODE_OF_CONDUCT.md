# Code of Conduct

Vazie is a small project, and the people around it — users, reporters and contributors — deserve the same respect the
code tries to show its users.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient. Many people here write in a second language.
- Assume good intent, and discuss the change, not the person.
- Keep issues and reviews on topic, and accept that the maintainer decides what fits the project.
- Respect privacy: never post anyone's passwords, configuration, subscription link, IP address or personal details.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination or sexualised language and imagery.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Spam, advertising and off-topic promotion.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and block people who do not follow these rules. To report a
problem, write to **support@vazie.app**; reports are treated privately.
